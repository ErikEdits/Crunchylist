Watchlog.

A private, temporary viewer for your anime watch history.

↑
Drop your CrunchyExporter data folder
choose files · choose folder — or just drop history.json
  • Works with exports from CrunchyExporter — drop the whole data folder or just its history.json. Other files (animelist.xml, export_log.json) are ignored.
  • Uploaded files are automatically deleted after a short time. Once that window passes, the link stops working and you have to upload again.
  • Everything is processed on the server only long enough to show it to you — nothing is kept permanently and nothing is shared.
  • Cover art is fetched from public anime databases (AniList / MyAnimeList) and cached in your own browser.

Open source — view the code on GitHub.
